# A source read after the forecasts were opened decides nothing in a registered analysis

Adjudication is blind to the forecasts, and the most authoritative source decides a trial's outcome (ADR-0008). The two rules collide after a reveal: once a trial's forecasts are public its two adjudicators have seen them, yet a paper or a regulator's review may still appear and be the more authoritative source. With only two adjudicators and no outside reader, nobody is left who can read it blind. So a reading made by someone who had already opened the trial's forecasts never decides a registered analysis. Where the trial has another source that was read blind, that reading stands and the later one is set aside and listed; where it has none, the trial has no result and holds up the final analysis until a blind reading exists. One sensitivity analysis repeats the primary comparison with the set-aside readings counted, so a reader can see whether later corrections would have changed the answer. Recruiting a third, permanently blind reader would keep ADR-0008 whole, but the study is run by two people and may not depend on a third. The cost is that a published correction after a reveal changes the scoreboard's footnote and the sensitivity analysis, not the registered result. Decided by the study lead on 2026-10-09.

Three refinements followed a review of the first implementation. Only a later source is set aside: one disclosed after its reader opened the forecasts. A source that was already public when the forecasts were opened, and was read afterwards, is simply not blind, and the trial is held back until a blind reading replaces it; otherwise recording that forecasts had been opened could be used to discard an unwelcome blind reading. For the same reason an opening is recorded on the day it happens and never back-dated. And a hazard ratio is a number copied from a source, not a judgement of whether the trial succeeded, so a hazard ratio reported by a set-aside source is still the one scored, and such trials are listed; without this the effect-size follow-up (ADR-0014), which runs after reveals have begun, would have nothing to score.
