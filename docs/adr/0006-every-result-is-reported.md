# Every result is reported, including a null

The registration commits Span to publish the outcome of the primary comparison whatever it is, including "the Span forecaster did not beat the base rate". A company would normally keep the option to stay quiet on a bad result; giving that up is what makes a good result believable, and a registered study that goes silent is worse for the company than the null. Whether to keep investing in the forecaster is a separate business decision, taken at the six-month look.
