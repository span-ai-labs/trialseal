# Span both runs the study and enters a forecaster

The study scores frontier models as shipped and also one named Span forecaster, frozen before each batch, with the conflict of interest declared in the registration. A neutral referee would be more credible and a Span-only study more useful commercially; entering both keeps the comparison fair while putting Span's own system on the record. Every safeguard that follows (sealing before readout, blind adjudication by two people, pre-registered rules) exists partly because of this conflict.
