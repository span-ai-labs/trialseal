# The effect-size analysis is completed six months after the final analysis

A hazard ratio is awaited for six months after a readout (ADR-0011), but the final analysis runs once, on a fixed date (ADR-0007). A trial that reads out in the last six months before that date may not have reported its hazard ratio yet, so at the final analysis it is listed as awaited and not scored. Leaving it there would drop the latest readouts from the key secondary analysis for good, and they are not a random subset. A registered follow-up therefore runs once, six months after the final analysis date. It scores the same trials the final analysis scored, exactly as the final analysis saw them, against hazard ratios disclosed by the follow-up date. It does not touch the primary comparison or any probability score. The alternative, restricting the effect-size analysis to trials that read out at least six months before the final date, was simpler but would have discarded about a third of an 18-month study. Decided by the study lead on 2026-10-09.

Because it is made once, the follow-up must not be made on an unfinished search: its first run requires the day on which the search for hazard ratios disclosed by its date was completed, and that day must be on or after the date. Where the final analysis made no claim, the follow-up reports estimates and tests nothing.
